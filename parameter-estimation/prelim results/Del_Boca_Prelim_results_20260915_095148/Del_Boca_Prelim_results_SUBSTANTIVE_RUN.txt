PRELIMINARY RESULTS: TAU WORKAROUND AND LIMITATIONS
Tau is interpreted as a parental-time share bounded between zero and one.
The exported Gaussian log-factor distribution can produce tau above one after
exponentiation. This run rejected the entire joint household draw whenever
any of its three log-tau values exceeded zero (equivalently, tau exceeded one),
then drew another household. It did not clip those values to one.
Of 38,305 proposals, 10,000 were retained (26.106%); 28,305 were rejected.
These are household-sampling acceptance figures, not Metropolis acceptance figures.

This samples the exported joint distribution conditional on feasible time shares.
It can change income, investment, child human capital, their correlations, and
mixture-component proportions as well as time itself. The procedure does not
repair the upstream Attanasio time measurement, distinguish child-directed time
from leisure, or re-estimate the production-function bridge. These estimates
should therefore be treated as exploratory, not final substantive findings.

The Excel Metropolis summaries use accepted proposals only and exclude repeated
states after rejections. Correct summaries should use the full saved chain in
the .jls file. The parameter-summary CSV reports optimizer estimates, not these
Metropolis averages. Inferential scaling also remains to be validated.
